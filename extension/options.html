Pickup Watcher settings

Status

Checks Apple Store in-store pickup for the products below and notifies you when one is in stock. Changes take effect when you save.

Where

Country store: leave empty for the US; otherwise the part of your Apple Store address after apple.com/, e.g. uk, ca, au. Only the US store has been tested. Distance is in the unit Apple shows for your country.

What

To find a part number: open the product's buy page on apple.com and choose the model, then open Chrome's DevTools (⌥⌘I), go to the Network tab and check pickup at a store. Look for a request named pickup-message or fulfillment-messages; its parts.0 parameter is the part number, e.g. MK1H4LL/A (%2F is a /). Up to 10 products.

Which stores
Checks

At least 60 seconds. Apple starts refusing checks (HTTP 541) within an hour at 30 seconds; 75 seconds ran for days.

Notifications

The key is the part after api.day.app/ in the push URL the Bark app shows.
